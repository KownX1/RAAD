Our Mission

At RAAD Consulting, we are dedicated to empowering individuals of all ages living with disabilities. Through personalized support, we foster growth and promote self-sufficiency.

We want to ensure our clients have access to all the important skills to thrive successfully. We pride ourselves by offering a variety of services tailored to individual needs.

Our Services

Application Assistance

Navigating the application process to access vital resources for individuals with disabilities.

Community Integration

Promoting full societal inclusion through community activities and social events.

Independent Living Skills

Developing essential skills to achieve greater independence in daily life.

Health & Fitness

Encouraging physical exercise with access to a fully equipped gym (no membership needed).

Parent Mentorship

Supporting parents and families through compassionate, hands-on mentoring.

Advocacy & Support

Guiding families in navigating complex state systems and accessing essential services.

Our Promise

Our loving expert consultants are dedicated to providing services to the Community with Love, Respect, Dignity, and Commitment.

Meet Our Founder

Angélica María Orellana

With over 25 years of experience working with individuals facing medical conditions, and as a devoted mother to a young man with special needs alongside two typical children, Angélica is deeply passionate about assisting those in need.

Her personal journey as an advocate for her son's education inspired her to create RAAD Consulting — a place designed with love and dedication to foster greater independence and enhance accessibility to essential programs for families.

Angélica's commitment extends beyond her own family, propelling her to serve others with the same passion and respect she desires for her own child.
